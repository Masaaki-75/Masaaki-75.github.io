01 / The interface

Many modalities, one interface.

Medical imaging offers complementary views of the body. MedVTok encodes 2D images and 3D volumes across nine modalities into a common token interface that downstream models can reuse.

02 / The approach

Many experts, one tokenizer.

Different medical imaging experts and foundation models capture complementary knowledge. MERA (Multi-Expert Representation Alignment) lets MedVTok learn from the specialist matched to each input. Shared-space alignment brings token and expert features together; alignment in the expert’s original space helps retain its distinct knowledge.

06 / Looking ahead

Toward a health world model.

MedVTok is a starting point for a broader goal: a coherent representation of a person’s health across modalities and over time.

We aim to connect medical images with clinical records and everyday health signals in a health world model that evolves as new observations arrive, supporting both patient care and lifelong well-being.
